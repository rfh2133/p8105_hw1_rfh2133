## Numeric Conversion

The logical variable converts to 1s and 0s because R treats TRUE as 1
and FALSE as 0. This explains why its mean can be calculated.

The character variable returns NA because letters cannot be converted
into numbers.

The factor variable converts to numeric codes representing its levels
rather than the actual category names.

These codes are just labels for categories, so calculating their mean
would not be meaningful.
